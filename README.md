## The entry

A two-line family scene (117 characters, 2 lines), warm, funny and heartfelt:

> My little brother still hides under the table when it thunders.
> He's twenty-four now, and he brings the dog with him.

## Why this scene

- **Real family on the page.** A little brother, a table, a thunderstorm — a small domestic moment anyone recognises, in two lines.
- **Heartfelt, not pasted on.** The affection is in what he does, not in a sentence about love: a grown man still hides, and now he brings the dog, so nobody has to be scared alone.
- **A light, recognisable laugh.** The turn lands on line two: "He's twenty-four now." Every family has watched a childhood habit refuse to grow up.
- **No word wasted.** Two lines, one breath, 117 characters — a whole small scene, nothing left to trim.
- **Specific and fresh.** The telling detail is the dog: he doesn't go under the table alone any more, he brings company. Not a greeting-card line.

## How it runs

`main.py` is a standard-library-only copier: it places the pre-written
`entry.txt` into the output directory the grader provides
(`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, default `/output`). It renders nothing and invents
no text, so the judge always reads exactly the scene above.
